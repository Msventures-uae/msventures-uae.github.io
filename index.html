About Us

An exporter of heavy equipment engine spare parts, based in Dubai

MS Ventures is an exporter of heavy equipment engine spare parts based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. From this leading regional trade and logistics hub, we serve customers across the GCC and Africa.

We specialise in OEM-quality aftermarket parts for Cummins, Caterpillar and Komatsu diesel engines. Our aim is to provide the correct part at a competitive price, sourced accurately by part number and supported by clear quotations with stated lead times.

We welcome your enquiries and look forward to serving you.

Our Customers

Industries Served

We serve businesses that rely on diesel engines operating for long hours, often at remote sites, where downtime has a direct cost. Our parts suit engines used in heavy equipment and power generation.

Mining and Quarry Operators

Mining and quarry operators and contract miners.

Typical needs: overhaul kits, turbochargers, fuel injectors and heavy-duty engine components.

Construction and Earthmoving Contractors

Construction and earthmoving contractors.

Typical needs: filters, pumps, gasket kits and engine parts for scheduled and breakdown repairs.

Equipment Rental and Power Rental Fleets

Rental companies operating mixed equipment fleets, and power rental and standby power providers.

Typical needs: cost-effective parts to return machines to service promptly; engine parts, filters, sensors and electrical parts for maintenance.

Telecom Tower Operators

Operators maintaining power equipment at tower sites.

Typical needs: parts for preventive maintenance and engine repair.

Parts Distributors and Engine Rebuilders

Distributors supplying workshops and end users, and workshops carrying out engine overhaul and rebuild work.

Typical needs: a dependable source of OEM-quality aftermarket parts for resale; complete overhaul kits and components, including hard-to-find items sourced by part number.
